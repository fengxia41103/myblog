Snow outside. It's rare that NC is painted white. Walked outside this
morning, taking some pictures. Reminded me much of the Boston days,
though, when in Boston, snow was such a commodity and annoyance, that
I don't recall when I went out just to take some pictures of it. Then,
here, it becomes a novelty. Is it? Or, am I just missing the days of
youth, of the good old days, of the days when there was hope, lights,
America was the America I liked, and future seemed bright and will
last?

The video is everywhere. I opened one, but then, I didn't want to, or
need to, watch it again &mdash; It is not only disheartening, it is
pure ugly, criminal, disgusting. If there is such as a thing called
**murder** in the book, this is the definition. If people back in the
1960s were shocked by the [Saigon Execution][1], well, I'm now
**shocked** that there could even be a _different_ interpretation by
[this woman][2] &mdash; How low can she go!? How low can America go!?
How low can humanity go!? This guy started by using his body to
protect a woman who was pushed down to the ground by force. Then, he
was muscled and shot in the head &mdash; he did every right move that
I have been teaching my son to do if he saw a woman being
bullied. Then!? This is the end of such act!? Are you fxxxking kidding
me!? How can we face our kids now and tell them what is right and
wrong!? How can they even tell now when there were a woman being
pushed to the ground!? Forget being a hero standing up to the
bullies. Here, the _authority_ is demo-ing the _correct answer_, with
a life taken away, with blood at their dirty hand, with a chilling
cheer and claps after the kill, with a [BS doll][2] standing in
front of the camera and lie, with a straight face.

Can there be anything worse than this!? I'm feeling suffocated
watching what is happening. At this point I am totally convinced that
there is no justice in this world &mdash; There is no way that that
fellow Jesus innocent if Roman could have had a news conference women
or ICE. Who said he was hang!? That's a straight lie!! He committed
suicide, and all because he was depressed by his wood works.

What is happening is nothing new. ICE is nothing new. They are the
American version of [boxers][3] &mdash; the lowest, uneducated thugs
living 200 years ago in one of the most backwards country in the
world, rose to power because of a political agenda, equipped with
hatred, and given the immunity to kill.. Isn't this identical!? Then,
fast forward 200 years, the same is happening, in the most advanced
country in the world, but still the lowest thugs that society could
have produced. How chilling when we see such parallel!!! Human didn't
advance at all!! The same evil. The same pattern. The same kill. The
same humanity shame. I used to feel bad that Cambodia had [Khmer
Rouge][4], and thought this was purely caused by the BS of the
revolution theory exported by the crazy [Mao][5]. What a bad luck.

But now, I don't feel bad anymore, not because I don't feel sorry
about such misery the people had been forced to witness, but because I
don't feel sorry for human as a race anymore &mdash; they are one ugly
kind of living form, that they are willing to **believe in whatever is
in the air**, and have no moral or bottom line of any kind. They are
capable to commit the worst form of crime, while their capability to
_think and debate and imagine and write and tell story and
reasoning_... all the used-to-be-the-best-aspect-of-a-human-attribute,
the crown jewel of human being civilized, different from other
animals.. is nothing but themselves an imagination &mdash; really,
human is living in its own _dream_. We think we are civilized, but we
are not. We think we are beyond the cruelty, but we are not. We think
we taught our kids lie is bad, but we did not. We think we have school
to churn out next generation with a basic moral standard of knowing
right and wrong, but we do not. We think we have a government system
that will not flip in a matter of months, but we do not. We think we
have a system that will prevent tyrant and kiss-ass public display and
putting-my-paycheck-above-my-duty elites, but we do not. We think we
have a group of smartest human being called the supreme court that
will brake on insanity, but we do not. We think we have hundreds of
millions of guns in private hands that no one can easily bully a large
number of citizens anymore, but we do not. We think we could disagree
and talk about it without violence, but we do not. We think we could

are just another neighbour and their kids go to the same school and
they are even a _good_ guy, but they are not. We think senates,

such criminal act, but they would not. We think the entire society
would be now condemning the killer now a gun shot is still ringing in

What is happening!? This is not the dream I was given, not the dream I
was having, not the world I thought I was living in, not the world I
want to live in. [Shame][7] on America. [Shame][7] on this
world. [Shame][7] on humanity.
