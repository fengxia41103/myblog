I found it inevitable that a person's thoughts are
self-contradicting. Though as hard as I try, I can not be consistent
in any way but keep having contradicting thoughts that translates
nothing but 既要又要还要就要. What a mess! This makes me wonder that
there is really no way for a person to be _honest_ even, that s/he
would have to admit that s/he **was wrong** before because the
thinking shifts to the opposite whether voluntarily or involuntarily.

I used to criticize the Chinese government for their hypocrisy. Yet I
found myself being just the same, that there is a dark side of me that
is not comfortable if the sun shines to expose. You could say this is
just human being. But it's a beast, one that is though checked right
now, is not removed, and surface often. For example, I find both a
pleasure to drive Noah back and forth though the trip is tedious and
tiresome. Nonetheless I treasure the time being with him even though
there was not much conversation, and I preached to himself and Mom
that he needs to be independent and give college life a run. Yet, I'm
so happy to see him, and miss him very much even just after dropping
him a minute ago! Is this hypocrisy? dishonest? that I just
_requested_ him to be tough and fly solo, criticized her being
spoiling him without boundary. Yet deep inside I'm glad he wants to
come home, he said he wants to live in my space if he is transferred
to Chapel Hill, he is there with me driving to school, we stopped by a
middle of nowhere (now I know a bit about the geo/location after this
drive, it's somewhere on Rt 8 before we got to Floyd, a seemingly a
tourist spot with a tiny main street which a car would pass in 1 min)
to enjoy the beautiful nature, he ran about quarter miles back to the
higher ground to take a picture... I truly enjoyed all these moments!
Thinking now that I won't see him till 11/20, a bit more than one
month, is actually not fun at all! I miss him, and wouldn't really
mind if I drive there every weekend, but but, that's what the Mom said
and I dismissed as being a bad idea! See!? This is just how
self-contradicting I am now finding myself into. It's a 人社崩塌.
Sigh.

AI. It's the buzz word for sure, and indeed this one is different from
all the previous waves in recent years &mdash; this one is shaking the
software development by lowering the bar from idea to a running
code. But also, I find it troublesome that I now don't know the code
intimately anymore, thus any small change gives you an uneasy feeling
if you skip consulting the **new oracle** for confirmation, and,
haven't we seen how often this same oracle was wrong!!!! Thus I
believe human has an inner requirement to have such a God figure,
some being that is beyond a person's comprehension, yet _it_ gives a
statement (Note: not a suggestion even!! It's always a statement,
affirmatively!) and we will feel now settled, assured, confident,
better than your own's word or judgement. Isn't this crazy!? This is
another example of self-contradicting of myself &mdash; I want to
establish an image of critical thinker, and sometimes even lean
towards being too judgmental. Yet, I have no issue to embrace the AI,
or technology, or science, or a book, a youtube, an idea, as if I
approve this message. But, what do I know about them, if at all!!! I
only justifed my adoption shallowly by some arguments so to convince
myself, and sometimes ppl around me, that yes I have gone through
critical thinking, and is only adopting them after such
examination. But in really, there is just explanation to pacify my own
uneasiness, to make myself feel **coherent/consistent**, which, if
taking a step away from your own entity, is no different than Nazi or
Communism or any those _high ideals_ proving themselves correct while
their practice resulted in human suffering and atrocity. Would my
double face lead to such disaster, too? I wonder. Right now I'm just
one nobody. Good. Limited power leads to limited damage. But what if
one day I became lucky, or unlucky I would think, that I were having
power?

Bad idea.
