It has been 3 months now. Susan's visit was timely, that she saved me
from drowning in an abyss of sadness. The time she was there was
lovely, fun, full of laughs, and full of life. But, deep inside, I
feel a void, a place that has been held up. I miss you, Friddie, if
only you could hear or understand, friddie, if you were there, here,
somewhere.

I couldn't comprehend how a little thing like you could have made me
feel so deeply wounded, and left such a void. When you first arrived,
for the longest time I couldn't find myself having the same type of
emotional attachment to you as I had to 猪猪, to 妞妞, to bunny. I
reasoned that you were not the original family, like you were a step
son, adopted, fostered, temporarily overlapping with me, I did the
duty to take care of you, but deep inside I didn't feel you touched me
at all.

But now, I start to feel it. I start to miss you, very, very, very,
much. I didn't know you had so much in me. I didn't know. I kept your
little bed, your pillow, your monkey, your little 罐罐, your
medicine.. every morning when I woke up, I imagined you came to me,
sitting next, waiting for me to get up and take you outside. I could
feel my hand brushing through your coarse hairs and your scarred
skins. I can see your pleasure to see me, I can hear you taking some
water with splashes all over. I wish you were here. I didn't know how
to take care of you, for a long long time, I didn't figure out the
lamb shank early, that could have been making you so happy, for much
much longer than you had had. I wish I knew all these. I wish I know
that your life is so short, so much shorter than mine, that each year
of your life is accelerated and condensed, like 10 of mine, that you
would have lost your sight, your smell, your energy, in merely six
years since you came to me, that the little one who was playing in the
snow, who was chasing squirrels in the campus yard, who was walking
with me on the trails and smelling here and there, would now be
gone. I didn't know. Now I do.

I regret, of me learning so slowly and so unwise to figure all these
out. For the first time in my life, I regret. I wish I had done more,
done better. I truly wish, I had loved you more, and knowing that you
loved me so, too. I didn't realize a little dog like you would have
left such an imprint on me. I thought you were so much less elegant
than Bunny, and not that even smart. But that was wrong. You had your
way, the silly, sincere, straightforward way, and that was precious.

Flipping through album on phone yesterday, I saw the video clip we
were at the ER, my hand caressing you, and you were lying there but
breathing hard and shaking. I felt a tremendous sadness swallowing
me. I did the right thing, I know, maybe the only way I know how, to
end your suffering. But, I miss you, Freddie, I do. Come and love me
once more, if you were still willing to, in another life, another
world.

Susan has a drawing of you, and I love it, as I wish I had known, how
I should have loved you, you little silly thing.
