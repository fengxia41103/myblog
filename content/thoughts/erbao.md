Never expected you coming into my life. Knowing you since 2020. Time
flies. Time heals. Time ferments you and me, started as we each a
separate entity, to now being interwined into a mix, 你中有我 我中有你.
When I was first introduced to you, I was drowning in an ocean of
sadness and loss &mdash; I left her in Shanghai, and I left 娃娃 at
home. I moved out to this place, with one only attachment that 娃娃
said that he liked this place, though I doubt he himself even
remembered that moment &mdash; He was sick and skipped school. To kill
time I booked a redfin tour to see this place. Never ever came to this
corner of Chapel Hill in my life before. This was the unit I booked,
then agent said there were other two places in this building. Both are
two bed, much more expansive, though looking great. This one, bare,
had nothing in it, looked a bit dated, conventional, and floor was not
even that clean. On the way back, 娃娃 sat in the back seat, I asked
him which one he liked the most, and he said "the first one". Well
well, that's the only one I could possibly afford anyway. But that
acknowledgement by him gave this place a meaning, and that set off
this new journey of my life.

I still recall the first night I spent here, not much furniture,
things, or even lights. I had one small clamp light from Ikea, the one
at the corner of the kitchen shelf still today, faint yellow light,
with a space like what was feeling inside &mdash; dark, vacant,
silent. I felt I lost all grasp of fun. I did not look forward to next
day, next year, next relationship, anymore. I had all the best, and
there won't be anyone better than that.

When first time talking to you, I still recall your nervous
voice. Selfishly I liked it, not because I liked the voice, but
because it satisfied my male feeling at the moment, when I felt I had
nothing to offer, nothing worth bragging, nothing to show off. Over
the Covid days we chatted sporadically. I never felt I would ever fall
for anyone any more, specially of a long distance relationship, the
last thing I would have ever wanted. I was tired, tired of trying,
tired of missing someone, tired of life &mdash; at the time, I had two
people in my heart, both perfect, and both far far away from me. I
don't know whether I would ever be able to love anymore, to miss
anymore, to hold someone dear anymore. I was scared.

One year, two years, three years, four, five... Time ticked away. You
stayed. We became acquainted. Our conversation developed from a
friend's hello to intimate talk, from kitchen, kids, to body, to
soul. From sending your daughter to England, to her coming back
home. From 娃娃 still a skinny kid fooling around first year of Covid
when he stayed a whole in this new place with me, to now he is 18 and
in college. We both grew old, I suppose, but you have not changed, as
if time had stopped if one is only to look at how constantly you have
been nurturing me, giving me courage, strength, confidence. Like
bringing a torch into a dark cold deep cave, it drove away the void I
felt inside, filled it with warmth, with light. I feel safe with you,
warm with you, peace with you.

Then, you drew 福福. It touched me deeply. Maybe it's God's will that
you came just at the time I lost him. Nothing could have been a better
testimony of love, whether it's a love of you to me, or of a love of
him, or a love as abstract word, than your drawing pen. You brought
him back to life, fixed him in time, and saved me in heart. I miss him
very much. I'm afraid I'll forget missing him one day. I'm afraid I'll
lose memory one day that I don't recognize him anymore and forget his
days. But now, I have you. I know you will remember him, I know you
love him, and you will, as always as you have done, to back me
up. This feeling of knowing that you will be there when I fail, is
real. It was said as a conventional wisdom that women beat men in
endurance, and some even say mental strength. For the first time in my
life, I started to understand it because now I can feel it, I can see
it, because you have it, and you gave it to me. What a bless! You say
you are lucky to have me. Not really. You are the better one, and I'm
the one who should be counted as lucky. There are plenty beautiful
face and body out there, but a golden heart is rare to find.

Finally, read your letter this morning. Not that I forgot, but I
wanted to save to a special moment, a moment I am not distracted by
other chores, so that I could think, only of you. Your aragami is as
lovely as always. As I said, you have a fun childish spirit at
heart. Is it a fox, or a cat? I would guess it's modeled after Bunny,
the other matching lovely spirit in this world as you. Your words gave
me too much credits. If only you knew how much you had brought into my
life, what I did when you were here was nothing in comparison. I
enjoyed the afternoon taking pictures in the back yard of OpenEye,
enjoyed a stroll on Franklin St. in the evening, enjoyed playing goofy
in the parking deck by the hotel, enjoyed your smile as an innocent
little girl, enjoyed your look of a movie star, a kitchen maid, a
classic chinese beauty, a tomboy wearing shoes like a lazy girl, a
tourist holding anything above the head just to avoid sunlight to age
your skin, a housewife hunting for a cover for your new bought frying
pan, a new chef experimenting with all kinds of 料理 as if for the
first time. That's life, a 生活 I like, 都因你才得.

Come soon. I miss you.
