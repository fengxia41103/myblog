Noah went to urgent care then ER yesterday. Didn't get home till
2:30am. Tired, perhaps, but it was not as tiring as I would have
thought. Spending with him is more precious now than ever because he
will be gone to college this year, and Paul and Barb keep telling me
that we are not gonna see him again anymore. I know I'll miss him,
regardless where he will be. Being able to still do something and
taking care of him actually made me feel satisfied, time well spent,
money well spent. Ann said the bill is not going to be cheap. Well,
we will deal with it when it comes.

Only slept for 5 hours, but actually feel quite good throughout the
day, brain was active, body wasn't too tired, finished a gym run, got
some work done, and here I want to write about a thought &mdash; watching the
我不是药神 movie again, and it makes me wonder that there should be at
least once in life that you need to do something that is not about
fortune and fame. Met a homeless on the street on Thursday. Well, I
guess he is a homeless, but honestly he didn't look like one from his
dress and watch. By finding him sitting outside a vape store, I would
rather he is more of a drug guy than homeless. Anyway. The usual
conversation that "excuse me sir" "sorry I don't have cash on
me". Actually I didn't have wallet on me at all. Was on my way to
check out the camera store since I was able to wrap up the day a bit
earlier than usual, I was hoping to hit the store so to take my mind
off work a little bit. "Can you buy me some food?" "Sure." He pointed
at the burger place, and another nearby restaurant, which I had passed
by million times, found it to be quite popular by noticing the cars in
the lot and crowds coming in and out during meal time, but I had never
stepped in. I figure it will be nice to check it out besides doing a
good will. "Can I get something for my wife, too?" "Sure." Why not.

The place is rather cute and spacious inside. I never realized there
is such a venue just next door. Anyway. So the guy ordered some food,
two drinks, $41. The cashier turned the kiosk to me, well, tips and
signature. 41! That's more than I treat myself even! and tips, for
take out!? Oh well well. I guess it isn't easy to be a nice guy
then. This reminds me the story Jessie Lee said that she offered a New
York homeless a burger, and the guy gave it back to her and said "I
don't eat beef" &mdash; what happened to the adverb of _beggars can't
be choosers_!? So much for a conventional wisdom. Now living in the
50s I am not naive to worship any such phrase anymore. All these wise
remarks sound great when rolling off your tongue. Just that there is
always another wise one having the exact the opposite meaning, can fit
in the sentence just as perfectly. So now you have a dilemma &mdash;
two wisdoms contradicting to each other, both can interpret the
context you are in seamlessly &mdash; which one? The correct answer
will be, **none**. Forget about smart remarks, conventional wisdom,
proverb. Do your own thinking, trust your own reasoning, use common
sense, and live in peace w/ yourself.

Back to the movie. I start to believe that a life worth living should
meet this condition, that once in life you should do something more
than fortune and fame, something that is, meaningful, something that
is beyond  material measure. I'm not talking about religion, but
something that you feel is worthwhile doing despite it brings you a
gain or not. He did it in the end because of a strong guilt when his
friend died. At this age I start to understand deeply how difficult it
will be when one can not reconcile with oneself, psychologically. So,
his move may look heretic, or desperate... doesn't matter what drives
him. His doing benefit all those patients who could not have otherwise
afforded the medicine. Even just as a side effect, what he did made
his life now, worthwhile.

Looking back at my choice of coming to DHHS and on the PATH project, I
still believe it's a privilege for me to be on this project, and be
able to contribute my learnings and skills and minds to something
which is far beyond a simple project. Even though everyday I'm
frustrated as hell, and sometimes even had the thought to quit, this
is a lucky move I made, without at the time even fully realizing the
meaning of it. But it makes my life now worthwhile. I don't know how
many kids are being helped by the system, or my work, directly or
indirectly. But I do believe this is a one of a life thing. When I
look back to my life years later, I would be proud of myself. I
remember in the 东京女子图鉴, one of the guys quit his office job and
went into non-profit. If you look around, all the great ppl you
admire, or the stories that touch you, all have this common, that the
person did something like this. Maybe that's what is called the moment
of the 人性的光辉. I don't know. But I do think this is important. We
seek meaning of life, and so many times felt defeated, wasted,
meaningless. If you feel that way, take a break from your routine, do
a change, change to do it once, once in life, of something you feel
to, you want to, despite its rewards. You will then find it.

I do think so.
