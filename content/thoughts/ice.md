Reading social media these days is depressing, not only politically,
but emotionally, and emotionally because of the politics. The whole
chaos of ICE in MN is not only disheartening. It is infuriating
&mdash; This is a showcase of a human brutality, insensitivity,
incivility, no shame, no conscience, and no heart.

The trauma is particular sharp because this is happening in America. I
recall at some point when I was young, facing the discussion that
America was abusing its muscle in the world stage by bullying other
countries, at the time probably was of Iraq invasion or some South
America invasion, I joked that, if these were indeed bullying, then
it's better to stand with the hooligan because he is practiced in
fight, thus stands a better chance to win. What a muddy head I was!

I still don't believe the cause is lost yet. VA's new governor just
signed to terminate cooperation of VA policy w/ ICE. Bravo to her!! I
have no idea who she is, what she stands for, preaches for, or the
agenda in mind. Still, bravo! This at least gives a clarion call to
today's USA, the State governments, that, do something. Didn't we
use the term **circuit breaker** in financial crisis to illustrate how
to stop propagation of a domino collapse!? Well, do something!!!! Do
something to contain this BS from going around, from spreading, from
continuing, from elevating the bottom of the human race to power, from
grabbing ppl off the street just because they don't carry their birth
certificate!? What is this prove-myself-innocent bullshit!!? How
ridiculous! After celebrating all these years of LMK, here we are,
just now targeting **brown** instead of **black**!? Are you kidding
me!? Is this just because you are color blind, or just that brown
isn't dark enough to be qualified as a _shade of black_!? Get a grip
ICE and Trump and MAGA. If you don't like dark skin, well, too bad,
that's God's "fault".

This whole idea of wearing mask is just pathetic and
wrong. Period. Watching the guy, what's his name again, Bovino,
walking in & out a gas station with a group of his boys is just like,
man, how scared you have to be in order to use a gas station bathroom
or shopping there!? Well well, if you are so scared, why go there at
the first place!? It must feel good to have armed guards around you as
if you were a king or dictator. But, when you spread fear to others,
yourself is also living in fear, aren't you!? What a twisted reality
this is. I read in a Reddit post of this guy that someone from his
college replied that he was actually a nice guy in school and
.... Sure, but it doesn't change a thing of what he is doing now. Just
that like that guy who shot Renee Good, his having a family or wife or
being a loving son or husband or whatever does not change a thing that
he was a lowest human being this human race has produced, and his fall
will come.

I'm not naive anymore to believe that bad guys will end badly. History
has shown plenty examples, otherwise. I don't believe in justice in
this life, because so many bad ppl got away from what they did, big or
small. Yet, one thing I believe, as a father, I believe to judge an
act being good or bad, just replace the other party, the one you are
speaking to, you are acting on, with your own child,
now ask yourself, will you say this to them? will you do this to them?
I respect [this father][1]. I have pictured myself in his situation,
what I should do? I couldn't think of a better answer, or ask, than
this. I'm not as brave as he is, but I wish I were. Stand up and speak
up. This is much needed now than ever, not for America, but for the
perseverance of humanity, for the hard-earned peace and kindness,
however small they have now become.

For your health, drink water, no ice.
